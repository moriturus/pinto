# **Decision**

Decision: Apply independent endpoint behavior to `sprint edit`, not `sprint new`.
Why: The Acceptance Criteria specify updating, while new Sprints have no counterpart to preserve and the persisted model requires a complete period.
Next verification: Focused CLI and service tests exercise endpoint preservation and reject incomplete or inverted effective periods.
