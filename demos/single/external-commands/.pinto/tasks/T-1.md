Package a `pinto-standup` executable that prints a daily standup summary and is
discovered Git-style when it is installed beside `pinto` or on `PATH`. Confirm
that `pinto standup` forwards its arguments, receives the `PINTO_DIR`,
`PINTO_HOST_VERSION`, and `PINTO_PLUGIN_CONTRACT_VERSION` contract environment,
and appears under `pinto help` with a concise summary.
